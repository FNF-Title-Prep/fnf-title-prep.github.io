# Colorado Title Insurance Exam Prep

A self-paced study site for the Colorado title insurance exam: a Guided Path, flashcards, quizzes, and full practice exams.

Open `index.html`, or visit the hosted site.

**Accounts (optional).** People can sign in with their work email so progress and stats follow them to any device, and managers get a view of the whole team. Setup takes about five minutes: see [docs/ACCOUNTS.md](docs/ACCOUNTS.md) and `supabase/schema.sql`. With `CLOUD` left empty in `index.html`, the site saves to the device only.

`assets/fonts/` holds the Inter typeface (SIL Open Font License; see `assets/fonts/LICENSE-Inter.txt`).
