About the real exam

The exam has 75 questions and a 75-minute limit. You need 70% to pass.

The questions come from a much bigger pool of hundreds, so every sitting is a little different. Some questions repeat from one sitting to the next and some are new, usually a mix of both.

The questions on this site are examples of what to expect, and many of them are likely to show up. But they are not a list of what's on the test. Learn the idea behind each answer, not just the answer.
